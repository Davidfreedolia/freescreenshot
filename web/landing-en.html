What you expect from a good screenshot tool

  • On-screen editor. Arrows, rectangles, text, numbers, marker, lines.
  • One-tap blur. Hide emails, keys and sensitive data before sharing.
  • Quick crop. Re-frame the shot after capture.
  • Pin on top. Keep a capture floating over windows as reference.
  • Built-in OCR. Turn text in the image into selectable text.
  • PNG or JPG. Pick the format and output folder.
  • Preview shelf. A floating thumbnail appears for a few seconds.
  • Hotkeys. Work from any app, even fullscreen ones.

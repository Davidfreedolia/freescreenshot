What is FreeScreenshot

A minimalist screenshot tool for Windows. Free, open source, gets out of your way.


Where it lives
==============

Once installed, the app lives in the system tray (bottom-right, near the clock).

If you don't see the icon, click the up-arrow (^) to expand the tray and drag the FreeScreenshot lime icon onto the main bar so it stays visible.


How to use it
=============

* Ctrl+Shift+1          → capture a screen area. Drag to select, Esc to cancel.
                          The screenshot is saved to Pictures\FreeScreenshot and copied to the clipboard.
* Right-click the icon  → menu with Settings, About, Donate, and Quit.
* Double-click          → opens the Settings window.


Privacy
=======

FreeScreenshot sends three optional signals to freedolia.com: the install ping, the silent version check, and the optional uninstall survey. You can turn all of it off in Settings → Privacy.
